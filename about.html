About

The story behind imageOmania

imageOmania is a creative platform that combines a powerful image search engine with an original design portfolio. It was built to give designers, photographers, and art lovers one place to find inspiration, explore stunning visuals, and discover hand-crafted illustrations that you won't find anywhere else.

Meet the creator

Hi, I'm Shailendra Mourya — a graphic designer and developer with a passion for illustration, visual storytelling, and building tools that make creativity more accessible.

imageOmania grew out of my love for both code and art. I wanted a single place where I could share my own illustrations while also offering a useful search tool for finding free photos and AI-generated imagery. Every design in this portfolio is hand-crafted — from the couple illustrations to the poster artwork.

I'm always open to collaboration, feedback, and new projects. Feel free to reach out at shailendramourya17@gmail.com .

What it does

What imageOmania does

Search

Powered by the Unsplash API and Lexica AI, the search tool surfaces millions of free photos and AI illustrations with powerful filters for orientation, color, and sort order.

Portfolio

21 original hand-crafted designs — couple illustrations, vector art, and poster work — all available to browse in the gallery with a full-size lightbox viewer.

Community

A collaboration space open to designers who want to share their work, get featured, and be credited. Submit your portfolio via the Collaborate page.
