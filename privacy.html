Overview

imageOmania ("we", "us", or "our") is a creative platform that provides image search and an original design portfolio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use imageOmania, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the site you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

Information you provide directly

  • Name and email address submitted through the contact form
  • Message content and subject line you send us
  • Portfolio links or files submitted via the Collaborate page
  • Newsletter subscription preference (opt-in only)

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URLs and search queries used to find the site
  • IP address (used only for security and analytics, never sold)

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Respond to your contact form messages and collaboration requests
  • Send occasional updates about new designs or features (if you opted in)
  • Understand how the site is used so we can improve it
  • Detect and prevent abuse or security issues

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

imageOmania integrates with the following external services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Unsplash API — powers the free photo search. Search queries are sent to Unsplash's servers. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.
  • Lexica — powers the AI illustration search. See Lexica's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions. Your name, email, and message are sent securely to Formspree. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces from Google's CDN. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Font Awesome CDN — loads icon assets. See Font Awesome's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

imageOmania does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. However, third-party services embedded in the site (Google Fonts, Formspree, Unsplash, Lexica) may set cookies or store data in your browser according to their own policies.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling third-party cookies will not break any core functionality of the site.

Data Storage & Security

Contact form data is processed and stored by Formspree on secure, encrypted servers. We do not store a separate copy of your message on our own servers.

The imageOmania site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. No user data is stored in a database operated by us. Search queries made through the Unsplash and Lexica integrations are not logged by imageOmania.

While we take reasonable measures to protect information, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that information
  • Opt out of any newsletter or marketing emails at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at shailendramourya17@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

imageOmania is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through the site, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of the page will change. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Significant changes will also be noted in the project's GitHub commit history.
