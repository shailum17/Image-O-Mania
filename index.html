Frequently asked questions

Can I use images commercially?
Most photos from Unsplash are available under the Unsplash License, which allows free commercial use without attribution (though attribution is always appreciated). Always review the individual image license before using it in a paid project.
Do you support AI illustrations?
Yes! Toggle the search type to Illustrations (AI) to search Lexica's database of AI-generated images. These are especially useful for unique, creative visuals that are hard to find with traditional photography.
How do favorites work?
Local favorites are coming soon — you'll be able to save and organise images directly in your browser without creating an account. Stay tuned for updates.
